Steel Salvo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Two Joes Gaming ("we", "us") publishes Steel Salvo, a turn-based tank artillery game for iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data We Collect

Steel Salvo does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs. All game progress (Gold, unlocked weapons, tanks, and liveries) is stored locally on your device.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (Gold packs, Remove Ads, and other items) are processed entirely by Apple's App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information — that is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Advertising

Steel Salvo is free to play and supported in part by optional rewarded ads and capped interstitial ads shown between matches. These ads are served through third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob), which may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional and never required to progress in the game. Purchasing "Remove Ads" disables interstitial ads.

Children's Privacy

Steel Salvo is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent via GitHub Issues.
