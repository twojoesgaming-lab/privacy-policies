Common questions

  • How do I fire? Drag on the battlefield to set your angle and power, then release — or use the ANGLE/POWER steppers and tap FIRE.
  • Is Gold pay-to-win? No. Every weapon, tank, and upgrade can be earned for free by playing. Gold only ever accelerates progress.
  • How do I remove ads? Purchase "Remove Ads" once from the Armory. It's a one-time purchase, no subscription.
  • How do I restore a purchase? Use "Restore Purchases" in the Armory screen.
