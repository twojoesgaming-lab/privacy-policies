Glomlings — Support

Glomlings is a drop-and-merge puzzle game from Two Joes Gaming. Drop round little monsters into a jar, match two of a kind to make a bigger one, and see how far you can grow before the jar overflows.

Need help?

If you've hit a bug, have a question, or want to request a feature, please open an issue and we'll get back to you:

Open a support request

Support requests are public. Please don't include your Apple ID, email address, order number or any other personal details. Your device model, your iOS version and what happened are all we need.

Common questions

  • How do I play? Slide your thumb anywhere on the screen to aim, then let go to drop. When two Glomlings of the same kind touch, they glom together into the next one up. The bubble marked NEXT shows which one is coming.
  • When does a run end? A dashed line sits near the top of the jar. If a Glomling that has already landed stays above that line for a few moments, the run is over. The line pulses to warn you first, so there is time to clear some room.
  • How do I score more? Bigger Glomlings are worth more, and gloms that follow each other quickly form a chain that multiplies the points.
  • What do the boosters do? Jar Shake jolts a stuck pile loose. Bonk Hammer removes one Glomling you tap. Glow-Up raises one Glomling you tap to the next kind. Second Wind is offered when a run ends: it clears the top of the pile and lets you carry on, once per run.
  • What are Coins? The in-game currency. You earn them at the end of every run, the first time you make each Glomling, and from the daily gift. You spend them on boosters, jar themes and pop styles. Buying Coins is optional; everything in the shop can be earned by playing.
  • What is the Glomdex? Your collection of the eleven Glomlings. Each one appears there the first time you make it.
  • What does Remove Ads do? It is a one-time purchase that switches off the full-screen ads between runs. Reward videos stay available, because you choose when to watch those.
  • How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase? Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases.
  • I bought Coins and they haven't arrived. Check that you are online, then close the game and open it again. If they are still missing, open a support request and tell us the day you bought them.
  • Will my progress move to a new device? Your Coins, boosters, themes and scores are stored on your device, and the game has no account or cloud save. A Remove Ads purchase can be restored on a new device; Coins cannot.
  • Do I need an internet connection? The game plays fully offline. An internet connection is only used for ads and purchases.
  • How do I turn off the sound, music or vibration? Open Settings in the game. Each one has its own switch.
  • The game only works in portrait. That's intentional. Hold your device upright.
