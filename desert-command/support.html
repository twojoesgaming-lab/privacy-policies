Common questions

  • How do I move the camera? Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, and twist with two fingers to rotate.
  • How do I win? Destroy every enemy production structure.
  • Do I need an internet connection? Not to play against the computer. Skirmish mode works fully offline. Online matches need a connection.
  • How do I play online? Open Multiplayer and tap Play Online (Game Center). You need to be signed in to Game Center on your device (Settings > Game Center). Matches are 2 to 4 players and are found automatically.
  • The game only works in landscape. That's intentional. Rotate your device to landscape.
