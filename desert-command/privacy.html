Desert Command — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Two Joes Gaming ("we", "us") publishes Desert Command, a real-time strategy game for iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data We Collect

Desert Command does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The iOS app does not connect to any server operated by us. Your settings (graphics quality, volume, and your last-used game setup choices) are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Online Play and Game Center

Online matches are provided by Apple's Game Center. Signing in, finding opponents, and connecting devices to each other are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. During an online match, the game exchanges in-game orders directly between the players' devices. We do not receive, store, or log that traffic. Other players in your match can see your Game Center display name, as they can in any Game Center game. Like any Game Center app, Desert Command asks Game Center who is signed in when it launches, so the standard Apple sign-in prompt may appear. Playing against the computer does not require a connection or a Game Center account.

Permissions

Desert Command does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or any other personal data on your device.

Purchases and Advertising

Desert Command has no in-app purchases and shows no advertisements.

Children's Privacy

Desert Command is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent via GitHub Issues.
