Flakline — Support

Flakline is an arcade defense game from Two Joes Gaming. Tap the sky to blow falling threats out of it before they level your cities, survive escalating waves, earn Scrap, and upgrade your interceptors between runs.

Common questions

  • How do I play? Tap anywhere in the sky. An interceptor launches from your nearest battery that still has ammo and bursts where you tapped; the blast destroys any threat it touches. The two buttons in the bottom corners are specials: tap one, then tap the sky to place it.
  • When does a run end? When all six cities are destroyed. Batteries that are hit stay down for the rest of the run, so protect them too.
  • What is Scrap? The in-game currency. You earn it in every run and spend it on interceptor upgrades between runs. Buying Scrap is optional; it only speeds things up, and nothing is locked behind money.
  • I can't hear any sound. Sound follows your iPhone's Ring/Silent switch: flip it to ring to hear the game. You can also check the Sound switch in the game's Settings.
  • How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase? Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases.
  • How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy & consent choices (shown where your region requires it). Tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Do I need an internet connection? The game plays fully offline. An internet connection is only used for ads and purchases.
  • The game doesn't rotate. That's intentional. Flakline is played in portrait.
