Common questions

  • How do I drive? Put your left thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen to steer; the car accelerates by itself. Pull the thumb down to brake or reverse. The BRK button locks the rear wheels for a handbrake slide. TURBO gives a burst of speed, FIRE shoots, WPN switches weapon, SPC uses your car's special move, and REAR looks behind you.
  • How do I win? Be the last car standing. Pick up crates for missiles, napalm, mines and more.
  • What are Bolts? The in-game currency. You earn them in every match and spend them on new vehicles and upgrades. Buying Bolts is optional; nothing is locked behind money.
  • How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase? Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Do I need an internet connection? The game plays fully offline. An internet connection is only used for ads and purchases.
  • The game only works in landscape. That's intentional. Rotate your device to landscape.
