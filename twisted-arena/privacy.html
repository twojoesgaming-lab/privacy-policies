Twisted Arena — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Two Joes Gaming ("we", "us") publishes Twisted Arena, a single-player vehicular combat game for iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data We Collect

Twisted Arena does not require an account, and we do not collect personal information. The app does not connect to any server operated by us, and we do not use analytics. Your progress and settings (your Bolts balance, the vehicles and upgrades you own, graphics and volume settings, and whether you have bought Remove Ads) are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

Twisted Arena is free to play. It shows optional rewarded video ads, and occasional full-screen ads between matches, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, ad networks such as Google AdMob may collect and use certain data, which can include your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it), your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. That data is handled by Google under its own policies, and we do not receive it. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: on iOS, the app asks your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites, and you can change that at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows a consent form before personalised ads, and you can review your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy Options. The one-time Remove Ads purchase removes full-screen ads; optional reward videos remain available because you choose to watch them.

Purchases

Twisted Arena offers optional in-app purchases (packs of Bolts, the in-game currency, and Remove Ads). Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store under Apple's terms. We never see or store your payment information. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase completed so it can add Bolts or remove ads on your device. You can restore a Remove Ads purchase on a new device in the app under Settings > Restore Purchases.

Permissions

Twisted Arena does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or any other personal data on your device.

Children's Privacy

Twisted Arena is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent via GitHub Issues.
